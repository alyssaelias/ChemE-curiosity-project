01 A note from Alyssa

I’m learning by
making things clear.

This project is my way of exploring chemical engineering before committing to it in college.

02 The starting point

Curiosity is a useful place to begin.

I keep noticing chemistry in things that feel completely ordinary: a soda going flat, soap lifting grease, a soft gel holding water. Chemical engineering seems to ask a bigger version of the same question — how do we understand these changes well enough to design useful things?

Before I make a big decision about what to study, I want to practice the work itself. Each episode is a small experiment in research, explanation, and reflection.

03 The process

Five steps from
question to reflection.

  1. 01

    Question

    Start with something ordinary that I genuinely want to understand.

  2. 02

    Research

    Follow the chemistry, read carefully, and keep track of what I learn.

  3. 03

    Script

    Turn a complicated explanation into a story another person can follow.

  4. 04

    Video

    Make a short, clear explanation and share it every two weeks.

  5. 05

    Reflection

    Write down what surprised me, what stayed confusing, and what I want to ask next.
